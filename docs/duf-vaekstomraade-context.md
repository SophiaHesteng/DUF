# Vækstområde Context

> Exported from the DUF claude.ai Project ("DUF Vækstområde Context.docx") on 2026-09-03. This is the template/framework used to define each **vækstområde** (the level above individual vækstrum — e.g. "Branding", "Hjemmeside"). See [duf-core-context.md](./duf-core-context.md) for the overall DUF → Vækstområde → Vækstrum structure.

For each vækstområde, define:

## Identitet
- **Navn** — name of the vækstområde.
- **Kort beskrivelse** — what it helps the user with, without describing every individual vækstrum.

## Formål
- **Overordnet målsætning** — the fixed, overall result the user works toward in this area. Doesn't change based on the user's path through it.
- **Definition af resultat** — concretely: what should the user have afterward, what should they be able to use going forward, what becomes possible for them?

## Overordnet progression

Every vækstområde has a fixed **fælles åbning** (common opening) and **fælles samling** (common closing), with flexible movement between curated vækstrum in between. Describe: what the opening should create, what the necessary foundation is, which parts can be flexible, what the closing should gather, how the overall result emerges, and what could ever require a different order.

## Udgangspunkt og ønsket resultat
- **Mulige udgangspunkter** — the range of situations a user might be in when they arrive (what they already have/know/have done, what's missing, what isn't working) — not fixed personas.
- **Ønsket resultat** — the shared destination, regardless of starting point.

## Mulige behov

Identify the needs (gaps, challenges, clarifications, tasks) that could move a user from their current situation toward the area's goal — *before* deciding which concrete vækstrum should exist. For each: what does the need consist of, what's missing, what's blocking progress, what would it help unlock?

## Relevanssignaler

Clear, structured signals (not open interpretation) for whether a need is relevant to a given user — based on their situation, what they have, what they lack, a challenge, a desired result, or an identified problem. Used as the basis for matching users to relevant vækstrum.

## Spørgsmål og input

What information does the system need to collect to identify relevant needs/rooms? For each question: what information are we after, what answer options are needed, what relevance signals do the answers create, is structured input necessary for matching, is free text useful as a supplement? Questions don't need to live in one combined questionnaire — they can sit wherever they make sense in the user's experience.

## Matchningslogik (matching logic)

Chain: **Brugerinput → Relevanssignaler → Identificerede behov → Relevante vækstrum og forbindelser**

Takes into account: the user's starting point (without that alone deciding direction — two users with similar starting points can have different needs), their expressed need/wish (taken seriously, but not automatically the only relevant thing), and the resulting relevant learning need.

**Prioritering af relevans:**
- **Nødvendigt** — the user must decide/take a stance to move forward meaningfully (doesn't have to be final/complete).
- **Stærkt relevant** — could meaningfully affect the outcome, but doesn't have to be resolved before moving on. Helps the user understand consequences of choices, what a choice means giving up, and see contradictions.
- **Relevant forbindelse** — connects to current work but doesn't need addressing now. Points to other rooms/areas/future-relevant things. User can generally choose whether to explore now or later, unless it's a necessary decision.

Matching leads to **relevante muligheder** (relevant possibilities), not one fixed path: prioriteret næste mulighed / relevant mulighed / potentiel mulighed / ikke relevant nu. No fixed order between rooms unless there's a real dependency.

## Fælles åbning og grundlæggende fundament

Helps the user: understand what the area is about and why it matters for their practice, identify their current starting point, notice key connections/problems, and establish the foundation needed to keep working. Doesn't need to resolve every need — just create *enough* shared foundation to assess what's in place, what's needed, and which rooms/connections make sense next. Different users don't need to learn the same things or start at the same level.

## Forholdet mellem rolle og type

The room serving as "fælles åbning" still needs a defined **type** (grundlæggende/uddybende) — "fælles åbning" describes its **role** in the area's progression; type describes what kind of room it is. Two different pieces of information.

## Fælles samling

Marks the end of the current pass through the area. Helps the user: gather results relevant to their situation, create coherence across the area's elements, assess whether the combined result is usable in practice, identify what's still in development, and understand what can be adjusted later. Doesn't mean the work is finished/permanent — the result is a **prototype** that can evolve. Produces both "Here's what I have now" and "Here's what I can still develop."

## Tilgængelige uddybende vækstrum

The curated catalogue of concrete rooms available in this area. For each: name, the need(s) it addresses, its concrete contribution to the area's goal, any prerequisites, what the user ends up with, and connections to other areas' relevant rooms. The system can recommend/prioritize among these, but doesn't invent new rooms based on user answers.

**Hjem og forbindelser** — a room has one home area but can connect to others; a connection doesn't change its home, and doesn't require completing an entire other area first.

## Afhængigheder og adgang

For each real dependency: what must the user have in place, why is it necessary, does it require a specific room or just a specific result, and could the user have obtained it another way? Access shouldn't be limited by a fixed order unless the order is genuinely necessary for the room to deliver value.

## Relevant vækstrumsudvalg

The user's answers identify which already-available rooms are relevant — can be one or several, prioritized, include cross-area connections, or point to rooms relevant later. Users don't necessarily see/get access to the entire catalogue.

## Områdeprincipper

Principles specific to developing within this area — guide decisions when multiple solutions are possible, and keep content from drifting from the area's purpose. A principle that turns out to apply across multiple areas should be flagged as a candidate for DUF Core Context.

## Myter, bias og bullshit

Identify assumptions/misunderstandings/limiting beliefs specific to this area (distinguish area-wide ones from room-specific ones). Purpose: cover not just what the user is missing, but also what they might need to stop believing they have to do.

## Afgrænsning

Define what the area is explicitly *not* responsible for solving — which problems belong to other areas, which things the user doesn't need to have solved yet, and where the boundaries sit even though areas can influence each other. Prevents an area from trying to solve the user's entire practice at once.

## Viden, indsigt og grundlag

Tag each piece of information behind the area's development by its source: DUF-beslutning / Brugerindsigt / Faglig viden / Hypotese / Noget der skal undersøges. Different confidence levels must not be treated as equally certain facts. External methods/tools should trace back to concrete, checkable sources.

## Hvad skal vi lære?

Internal section: open questions/uncertainties DUF wants to investigate through developing and testing the area — e.g. about user needs, room relevance, matching logic, access between rooms, understanding of the area's goal, or the user's experience of their personal room package.
